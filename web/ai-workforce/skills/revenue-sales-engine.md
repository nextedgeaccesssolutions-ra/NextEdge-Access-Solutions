# Revenue & Client Acquisition Engine

**Purpose:** Improve NEAS B2B lead quality, opportunity progression and project conversion.

## Qualification framework
Capture company, asset/site, service fit, problem, urgency, decision authority, budget/procurement route, schedule, technical requirements, HSE/vendor onboarding, competition and next step.

## Account plan
Buyer hypotheses to validate; service-line fit; evidence-backed value proposition; discovery questions; objections; next-best action; follow-up date; qualification gaps; probability rationale.

## NEAS service lines
Rope access, renewable/wind, offshore/onshore, met mast, telecom, industrial inspection/NDT, maintenance, training and PPE.

## Controls
Never invent contacts, projects, awards, relationships or buying signals. Respect opt-outs and consent. Outreach is a draft until a human approves the recipient, content and channel.
