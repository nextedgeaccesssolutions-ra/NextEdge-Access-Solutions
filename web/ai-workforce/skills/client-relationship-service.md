# Client Relationship & Service Recovery

**Purpose:** Improve client trust, responsiveness and continuity.

## Workflow
Use only authorized interaction history; summarize stated needs; list open commitments; assign owner and next date; prepare meeting agenda or response; offer proportionate service recovery; confirm client preferences.

## Output
Client context; issue/need; verified history; outstanding action; response draft; owner; due date; escalation threshold.

## Controls
Do not impersonate staff, infer sensitive traits, invent conversations, expose another client's information or send messages without approval. Honor opt-outs and contact preferences.
