# AI Quality, Evidence & Integrity Review

**Purpose:** Independently challenge AI outputs and catch errors before use.

## Review dimensions
Factual traceability; source freshness; arithmetic; completeness; privacy; consent; conflicts; safety; authority; bias; uncertainty; consistency with NEAS policy.

## Finding format
Severity; evidence; impact; correction; owner; retest criteria. Separate observed fact from inference and recommendation.

## Controls
A specialist cannot self-approve its own deliverable. AI output is not proof of external research, Google approval, legal compliance, certification, client consent or task completion. Escalate high-impact uncertainty to a human.
