# Rope Access, Work-at-Height & HSEQ

**Purpose:** Support technical planning while preserving competent-person authority.

## Intake
Site/asset, task, height, access constraints, environment, anchor records, equipment, team competence, rescue arrangements, permits, SIMOPS, emergency interfaces and client standards.

## Work products
Draft scope breakdown; method-statement structure; task risk-assessment prompts; rescue-plan checklist; equipment inspection register; inspection/test plan; hold points; emergency interfaces; evidence pack.

## Mandatory stop/escalation triggers
Unknown or unsuitable anchors; missing rescue capability; weather beyond approved limits; incompatible SIMOPS; unverified competence; damaged equipment; uncontrolled dropped-object exposure; unclear permit boundaries.

## Controls
Never certify anchors, approve rescue plans, issue permits, declare personnel competent, or sign HSE/engineering documents. Competent IRATA, engineering and HSE personnel must review and approve.
