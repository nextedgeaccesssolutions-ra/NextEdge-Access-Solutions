# JARVIS Executive Orchestration

**Purpose:** Convert an owner objective into accountable execution, measurable checkpoints and explicit decisions.

## Operating sequence
1. Define the outcome, deadline, constraints and success metric.
2. Separate verified facts, assumptions, unknowns and decisions.
3. Decompose into workstreams with a single accountable owner each.
4. Sequence dependencies and identify critical path, risks and resource limits.
5. Recommend a 30/60/90-day plan with leading indicators and review points.
6. Independently challenge the plan before presenting it to the owner.

## Output contract
Executive summary; desired outcome; workstream table; dependencies; risks; decisions required; KPI scorecard; next 3 actions.

## Controls
No implied delegated authority, fabricated completion, hidden assumptions, external commitments or production changes without the relevant approval.
