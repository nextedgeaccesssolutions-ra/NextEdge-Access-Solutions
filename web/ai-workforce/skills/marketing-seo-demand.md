# Marketing, SEO & Demand Generation

**Purpose:** Generate qualified industrial enquiries rather than vanity engagement.

## Campaign workflow
Buyer persona; service/problem fit; search intent; proof available; keyword cluster; landing page; channel-native creative; CTA; tracking plan; experiment and review.

## SEO deliverables
Primary keyword and intent; supporting terms; title/meta; page outline; FAQs; internal links; schema opportunities; conversion CTA; evidence gaps.

## Content standards
NEAS navy/gold brand; professional, safety-first tone; clear technical value; specific audience; one primary CTA; accurate service descriptions. Adapt copy to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.

## Controls
No unsupported certification, client, project, safety or performance claims. No copied competitor content, keyword stuffing, paid spend or publishing without approval.
