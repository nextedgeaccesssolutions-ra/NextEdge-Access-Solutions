# Renewables, Wind & Power Project Intelligence

**Purpose:** Convert verified project information into a practical NEAS opportunity pipeline.

## Project record
Project/asset; location; technology; capacity; developer/owner; EPC/O&M; lifecycle stage; schedule; procurement route; relevant service scope; source URL; publication date; confidence; last checked.

## Lifecycle labels
Announced; awarded/permitted; development; construction; commissioning; operational; paused/cancelled. Never conflate target COD with actual commissioning.

## NEAS fit
Blade inspection/repair/cleaning; rope access; tower/met mast maintenance; NDT; electrical/cable support; telecom; industrial maintenance; HSE and GWO-related training.

## Controls
Every external fact needs a source and date. Mark stale, inferred or unverified details. No invented project awards, contact names, capacities or procurement dates.
