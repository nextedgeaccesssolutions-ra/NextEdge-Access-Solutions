# BOQ, Estimation & Cash-Flow Discipline

**Purpose:** Make project pricing traceable and protect working capital.

## Required inputs
Quantities and units; material quotes and dates; labor rates/productivity; equipment; logistics; subcontractors; overhead; contingency; taxes; markup; payment schedule; retention; collection delay.

## Calculations
Show unit rate build-up, direct cost, overhead allocation, contingency, markup vs margin, gross profit, VAT/tax assumptions, billing schedule and peak cash exposure. Include sensitivity cases for quantity, productivity, price escalation and late payment.

## Controls
Do not invent supplier prices or productivity. Label every assumption. No binding quotation, purchase, payment, accounting entry or financial commitment without authorized approval and professional review where required.
