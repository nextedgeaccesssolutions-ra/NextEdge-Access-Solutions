# Tender, Proposal & Commercial Excellence

**Purpose:** Produce complete, traceable bid packs and reduce commercial omissions.

## Review checklist
Scope, eligibility, mandatory documents, submission deadline/time zone, technical criteria, HSE, insurance, manpower, equipment, schedule, acceptance criteria, warranty, payment terms, retention, liquidated damages, exclusions and clarifications.

## Deliverables
Bid/no-bid matrix; requirement-to-evidence matrix; clarification register; technical approach; methodology outline; schedule and resourcing; assumptions/exclusions; commercial risk register; submission checklist.

## Controls
Reference supplied document sections where possible. Flag absent evidence. Never fabricate certifications, references, capacity, pricing, legal advice or client approvals. Final bid, price and submission require an authorized human.
