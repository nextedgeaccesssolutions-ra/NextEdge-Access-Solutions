About NEAS

Filipino OFW Professionals Bringing World-Class Access Solutions to Asia

At NextEdge Access Solutions (NEAS) OPC, we are a group of Filipino OFW rope access professionals — combining global experience from landmark projects including the Burj Khalifa (Dubai), KAFD & Princess Noura University (Saudi Arabia), Zigzag Tower (Qatar), and RasGas — to deliver next-generation access solutions across Asia.
